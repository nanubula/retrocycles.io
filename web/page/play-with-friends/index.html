Play with friends

A 1v1 with one link, or the same room for everyone.

Your friends need only a browser: no download and no account. Send them a link, and they play with you.

Challenge a friend to a 1v1

  1. On the home screen, press Challenge a Friend.
  2. Copy the invite link, or share it, and send it to your friend. The link expires after a short time.
  3. Your friend opens the link. When you are both there, the match starts in a private room.

The match is a Sumo duel: you score a point when your friend hits a wall, and the first to 12 points wins. At the end, you can both press Rematch to play again. Other people who open the link can watch.

Invite friends to your room

In a game, open the menu with Escape (or the menu cell on a phone) and choose Invite. Copy the link and send it to your friends. The link takes them to your room, if it has space.

With an account

Friends need an account: sign in, or save your progress, which creates one. Then you can:

  • add friends, and see who is online and where they play;
  • join or watch a friend's room;
  • challenge a player in your room to a 1v1 from their card.

You decide who can challenge you. In Settings, you can turn challenges off or block a player's challenges.
